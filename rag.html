The mental model to remember

RAG is not one model. It is a pipeline that combines retrieval with generation.

Retrieval Find the most relevant pieces of information from your external knowledge source.
Augmentation Put the retrieved information into the LLM's context along with the user's question.
Generation The LLM uses that context to generate the final response.
